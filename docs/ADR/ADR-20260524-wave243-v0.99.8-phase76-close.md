## Karar

### 76.A — `cd/audio/Compressor.hpp`
Feed-forward compressor:
- envelope (attack/release) > threshold → `(env/T)^(1/ratio)·T` gain
  reduction.
- one-pole smoothing on gain (soft knee).
- `prepare(rate, threshold=0.5, ratio=4, attack=5ms, release=100ms)`.
- `gain_db()` — telemetry / meter.

Limiter (Phase 41) hard ceiling; Compressor soft musical dynamic
shaping (dialogue bus, music bus).

### 76.B — `cd/scene/LightProbe.hpp`
First-order spherical harmonic ambient probe:
- 9 SH9 RGB coefficients (27 floats).
- `evaluate(probe, normal)` → Vec3f irradiance.
- Ramamoorthi & Hanrahan diffuse SH basis (Y_0_0, Y_1_-1, ..., Y_2_2).

Cluster/PBR shading'in low-frequency ambient katmanı. Probe volume
interpolation caller işi.
